Runs stop paying for walls and process. A lane's Owns is a hint: a worker changes whatever its task needs and reports each file outside its Owns on an `EXTRA:` line with its reason, which the reviewer reads; the test guard and the `out_of_scope` stop are gone. A stop that names a sibling's file waits for that sibling instead of being dispatched again. Lanes talk through a team channel (`catherd team send|read|wait`, and the same tools on the role server, after T3 Code's thread tools): messages between lanes are queued, and the architect's and the owner's reach a running opencode lane at once. No gate judges an acceptance line about commit messages, test-first evidence or file scope. The architect may add, change, split or drop tasks, lanes and milestones mid-run with no approval park. A red Check gives at most five findings per item. The decision model's fallback picks the lowest level that is enough, summed from the bottom. A fresh milestone tree's submodules borrow objects from the main checkout instead of cloning them again from the remote.

Cursor is retired: a profile that names a Cursor rung loads with it dropped and a note.
