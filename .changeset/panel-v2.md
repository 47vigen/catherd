The panel, v2. A dashboard of every run on the machine: what runs now, what waits for you, and KPIs and charts for the last 7 or 30 days. Each run is a live graph from the plan through every milestone step; any node opens its prompt, its log as a readable transcript (or raw), its output, its diff and its checks, with model, time, tokens and cost. Pause and resume one run, cancel one role. Setup edits every profile setting for any repo, shows the change before it saves, and opens at once from a probe cache. Questions show their context and one-click answers.
