The cheap tier. opencode: a mid-run abort that the agent recovered from ends `ok` when the reply has its `STATUS:` line; a run whose model has no price in opencode's own figures is priced from the catalog; its transcript shows as the other backends' do. Routing: a lane on the critical path of the remaining plan starts no lower than the role's default rung, a lane with slack is held to the next easier difficulty's bar, and a `Chore: yes` lane starts on the cheapest capable rung. A cheaper rung with at least as good a record on this machine (5 or more finished lanes of that difficulty) is preferred, and the why names both records. A fix round of only lint, type-check or naming findings runs once on the cheapest rung first. `catherd catalog outcomes [--json]` prints each rung's first passes, rounds and findings per lane.
