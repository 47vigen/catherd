Part of 2.0.0. The terminal dashboard is removed, with its `@opentui` dependencies. `catherd panel` serves a browser panel on 127.0.0.1 behind a one-time token: Setup (backends, the catalog, each role's rungs from indexed Slopalytics pairs), Runs (every run and its live timeline) and Questions (answer a parked unit). A bare `catherd` opens the panel and `catherd --help` prints the help; `catherd watch` prints text only.
