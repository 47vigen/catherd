Panel polish from a live review. The run graph's zoom, fit and full-screen controls are a small overlay; the graph pans by dragging with the mouse, and in full screen it fills the dialog, zooms with a trackpad pinch around the pointer and keeps zoom there only. The Models page is the chart itself, edge to edge, with the title, axis switch and model list floating over it; a lab's models share its colour, deeper for the stronger ones, every line solid with hollow dots, a crosshair to both axes, the tooltip of the dot under the pointer, and the list strongest first. Setup's model picker lists one row per model with its efforts and their scores, with the search, a provider filter and Add fixed while the list scrolls. Cursor shows its own mark.
