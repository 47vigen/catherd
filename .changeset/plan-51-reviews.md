Converging reviews (plan 51). A reviewer reports IMPORTANT, NIT or PRE-EXISTING findings with their bug class; a review runs on another model family than the work it reads (marked same-family when its ladder has none); a re-review of a ledger task reads only the diff since the last one, and a fourth round is refused until the task is replanned. The architect logs each finding's handling and missed bugs with the new `findings` tool, and the MR's Known issues list what is left. The `ui-reviewer` role folds into the reviewer: profiles migrate once, and the reviewer's default access is `full`. New: `catherd runs metrics`, a Metrics tile on the run page, and `catherd scorecard stage` for a per-task stage comparison. MIGRATION.md, "Converging reviews and the ui-reviewer fold (plan 51)".
