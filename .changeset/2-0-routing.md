Part of 2.0.0. Routing on one scale: Jev's yes/no answers floor a lane at logic (an unexplained cause, a cross-service invariant, shared state) or cap it at build (purely mechanical work). The shipped bars are the 20th, 45th, 60th and 65th percentiles (copy, build, logic, hard), and a profile or a single role can move one (`bars`). `maxEffort` caps a model's effort for every route, climb and failover; the default profile caps Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 at high and GPT-6.1 Sol at xhigh. Coding workers never run below medium effort, and no objective or budget puts cost ahead of that floor. Every failover is a `failover` row in `routes.jsonl`.
