# catherd 2.0, plan 33: routing on one scale — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the routing half of decision 11 and the rest of decision 6, on the single intelligence scale plan 29 leaves. Jev's yes/no answers floor a lane at logic and cap it at build. A profile caps a model's effort (`maxEffort`; the default profile ships Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 at high and GPT-6.1 Sol at xhigh), the shipped bar percentiles become copy 20, build 45, logic 60 and hard 65, and a profile or a role sets its own, one difficulty at a time. A coding worker never runs below medium effort, and cost never comes ahead of that floor in a route, a climb or a failover. Every failover is a row in `routes.jsonl`.

**Architecture:** Every rule is pure and lives in the domain. `judgeRoute` (`src/domain/jev.ts`) applies `route-v2`'s floor and cap from `catalog/jev.json`. `effortAllowed` (`src/domain/select.ts`) drops a rung above its model's `maxEffort`, or a worker rung below `medium`, inside `candidates`, so every route, ladder, climb and `rolesWithoutRung` sees only what remains. `percentilesOf` (`src/domain/bars.ts`) lays a role's and a profile's `bars` over `BAR_PERCENTILE` before `barOf`. Two services change. Plan 30b's `failoverRung` filters its pool through `effortAllowed`. Plan 30's `dispatch` effect writes a `FailoverRouteRow` whenever it fails a unit over. No event type, action type or service is added.

**Tech Stack:** Bun ≥ 1.4, TypeScript, zod 4. No new dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-12-catherd-2.0-design.md`, decisions 6 and 11 (the routing half), and the routing line of 22. Bound by plan 29 (`docs/plans/2026-10-21-29-shrink.md`), plan 30 (`docs/plans/2026-10-21-30-run-daemon.md`) and plan 30b (`docs/plans/2026-10-21-30b-unattended.md`): their Produces blocks and Rulings. ## Review Focus

1. **A worker whose every rung sits above its model's `maxEffort` or below medium.** Expected: `profile validate` names each rung and says routing skips it. `rolesWithoutRung` names `worker`, so `run_start` refuses the run at start and never fails it midway (decision 12). Pinned in Task 2: "rolesWithoutRung names a worker whose every rung is above maxEffort or below medium".
2. **Budget pressure, or `objective: "speed"`, with a cheap or fast low-effort worker rung that clears the bar.** Expected: the worker never starts on it, and a writer still may. Pinned in Task 2: "a worker never starts below medium, whatever the objective" and "under budget pressure a worker still starts at medium or above".
3. **A dead-band Jev answer with a sure yes.** Expected: a yes to `mechanical` holds the lane at build. A yes to an unexplained cause lifts it to logic, and the floor wins over the cap. Pinned in Task 1: "decides a dead-band lane by a yes at exactly noulMin, and the floor wins over the cap".
4. **A hand-edited profile with `maxEffort: { "codex:gpt-6-sol": "turbo" }` or `bars: { hard: 150 }`.** Expected: the profile reads, the bad entry caps or sets nothing, and validate warns how each entry is read. Pinned in Task 2 ("reads a ceiling it does not know as no ceiling, and warns") and Task 3 ("drops a stored bar it cannot read, and warns").
5. **A failover whose nearest stand-in is above `maxEffort`, or below medium for a worker.** Expected: that stand-in is never started, and the next one is. Every failover, chosen or none, leaves a `source: "failover"` row that `readRoutes` and `readRoleRoutes` skip. Pinned in Task 4 ("never fails over above maxEffort, nor a worker below medium") and Task 5 ("writes a failover row …", "readRoleRoutes and readRoutes skip failover rows").

## Rulings

`Ruling: <what> — <why> — <cost if wrong>`:

1. **Ruling: nothing that plans 29, 30 and 30b deliver is rebuilt.** That covers the hold (30 D9), the configured-backend pool, never-down, Claude-last (30b Rulings 9–10), percentile bars (29 Ruling 1) and the one-scale ladder (29). The draft's `limits.json`, `moveOffDown`, `nextStandIn` and `failover()` chain are dropped, because the daemon's `holds` and `failoverRung` replace them — the brief says plan only the gaps — cost: none.
2. **Ruling: the floor and the cap also apply in the dead band, and when Jev gave no difficulty.** A sure yes moves a lane with no track to `logic` or `build`. The floor wins over the cap. `rule` gains `; floor logic: <id> <p>` or `; cap build: <id> <p>` only when the difficulty changed — t3herd `judgeAll` does the same (draft Ruling 4) — cost: a dead-band lane that fell to the default rung now routes as `jev`.
3. **Ruling: `noulMin`, `floorLogic` and `capBuild` give `route-v2` a new `questionSetId`, and leave the request cache key alone** — `questionSetId` hashes the rule and `requestKey` does not (draft Ruling 5) — cost: none.
4. **Ruling: a lane that declares both `Kind:` and `Difficulty:` keeps its declared difficulty. The floor and cap shape only a difficulty Jev decides, and `jevSaid` names Jev's adjusted answer where it differs** — 1.5 plan 24's "a declared header wins", and plan 32 makes lanes from reviewed plan tasks — cost: an under-declared lane gets no floor.
5. **Ruling: `bars` is a set of percentiles (0–100), one per difficulty, at two levels. `roles.<role>.bars` lies over the profile's `bars`, which lies over `BAR_PERCENTILE`, one difficulty at a time** — decision 11 calls bars percentiles and lets "a role … set its own". The profile level gives back what `catalog.override.json` offered before plan 29 removed it (29 Ruling 14) — cost: a user who thinks in intelligence points converts them to a percentile.
6. **Ruling: the shipped `BAR_PERCENTILE` is copy 20, build 45, logic 60 and hard 65** (plan 29 shipped 25/50/60/75). On this plan's spike of plan 29's 2026-10-13 pool (81 pairs of the 20 fixture families) they map the default worker one rung per difficulty: copy Luna high, build Sol medium, logic Sol high, hard Sol xhigh — closer to 1.5 and to t3herd's tuned bars, and less Codex quota on copy and build. The values stay configurable per profile and per role (Ruling 5). Every test in this plan pins its own `indexOf` pool, and every bar it names is computed with these percentiles; a plan-29 test that pins a bar or a route on `fixtureIndex()` is re-derived at execution as `barOf(shipped({ index: fixtureIndex() }), d)` (Task 3, Step 3) — owner answer, 2026-10-21 — cost: a hard lane clears on a weaker rung than plan 29's bars allowed.
7. **Ruling: `maxEffort` is profile-level and keyed `<backend>:<model>` (`"codex:gpt-6.1-sol": "xhigh"`).** Resolved, it holds only `EFFORT_ORDER` words. It filters `candidates` (so every route, ladder, climb and the start check) and `failoverRung`'s pool and explicit stand-in. A rung whose effort is not an `EFFORT_ORDER` word (`#default`) is never capped — t3herd `capPairs`, draft Ruling 16 — cost: none.
8. **Ruling: the default profile ships `DEFAULT_MAX_EFFORT`, t3herd's caps under the shipped catalog's ids on every backend the catalog lists them on:** Claude Opus 5.5 at high (`claude:claude-opus-5-5`, `claude-code:claude-opus-5-5`, `opencode:opencode/claude-opus-5-5`, `cursor:claude-opus-5-5`), Claude Sonnet 5.5 at high (`claude:claude-sonnet-5-5`, `claude-code:claude-sonnet-5-5`, `opencode:opencode/claude-sonnet-5-5`), GPT-6.1 Sol at xhigh (`codex:gpt-6.1-sol`). No default rung exceeds them, so the default profile still validates clean, and 30b's any-backend failover can never start a `#max` Opus — owner answer, 2026-10-21 — cost: a user who wants Opus at xhigh removes the cap (`catherd profile set maxEffort.claude-code:claude-opus-5-5 null`).
9. **Ruling: the "coding workers" of decision 22 are the `worker` role (`CODING_ROLES = ["worker"]`).** That role runs repo_code, terminal and ui lanes, every fix round and `worker-<M>-integrate` (plan 30 Ruling 10, 30b Ruling 14). Writer, researcher, artist, reviewers, verifier, architect and judge are left out — the decision says "coding" — cost: none, since plan 30 runs image lanes as worker lanes too.
10. **Ruling: the floor is a filter in `candidates` and in `failoverRung`, applied before any ordering.** So cost order, `objective: "speed"` and budget pressure (`BUDGET_CHEAP_AT`) only ever see rungs at medium or above — "routing does not put cost ahead of that floor" — cost: none.
11. **Ruling: an effort that is not an `EFFORT_ORDER` word (`default`) passes the floor** — it is the model's own setting, which catherd cannot rank — cost: a worker ladder that lists a non-reasoning `#default` model runs it, and validate says nothing.
12. **Ruling: a rung above `maxEffort`, or a worker rung below medium, is a validate warning, not an error.** A role left with no usable rung fails through the existing paths: `noRung` in `select`, validate's zero-usable error, and 29's `rolesWithoutRung` at run start — the same treatment as `listed: false`, and decision 12's "stops the run at start, never midway" — cost: none.
13. **Ruling: `EFFORT_ORDER`, `EffortWord` and `effortRank` move to `src/domain/ids.ts`, since plan 29 deletes `src/domain/sources.ts`.** If plan 29 kept them elsewhere, import them from there and add nothing — cost: none.
14. **Ruling: the `dispatch` effect writes one `FailoverRouteRow` each time it runs with `failoverFrom` set, after `failoverRung` answers and before admission.** It writes the row when a stand-in is chosen and also when none is (`rung: null`, then `E_ALL_HELD`). A failed append is logged and never fails the dispatch — plans 30 and 30b write none, and `routes.jsonl` is where catherd keeps routing decisions — cost: a long hold writes one row per wake (one every 30 minutes at most).
15. **Ruling: `readRoleRoutes` keeps only `source` `"route"` and `"dispatch"` rows** — a lane-less failover row has `lane: null` and would read as a role route — cost: none.
16. **Ruling: a pending lane whose own rung sits on a held quota waits for the hold, because plan 30's advance dispatches only when the "quota free" condition holds. It is not moved off the quota** — decision 6 speaks of a limit, and the lane that hit the limit fails over at once — cost: a lane routed onto a held quota waits up to 30 minutes. Recorded in `ideas.md` (Task 6).
17. **Ruling: the panel (plan 31) neither shows nor edits `maxEffort` or `bars`; `catherd profile set` and the session's `profile` tool do** — plan 31's Setup page covers backends and rungs — cost: none. Recorded in `ideas.md`.
18. **Ruling: `catalogQuery` still lists below-medium efforts under the worker role, and validate warns when one is picked** — the listing describes the models, and the floor is routing's rule — cost: the panel offers a worker rung that never runs.
