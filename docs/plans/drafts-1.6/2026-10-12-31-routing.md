# catherd 1.6, plan 31: routing — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Jev's yes/no answers move a lane's difficulty (a floor at logic, a cap at build); a profile can cap a model's effort (`maxEffort`) and give a role its own bars; a provider that hits a limit is remembered as down, `route` starts a lane off it, and failover keeps going (a provider error retries once, then the profile's stand-in, the role's ladder, the profile's rungs and the catalog, past every stand-in that is limited too), each failover written to `routes.jsonl`.

**Architecture:** The rules stay pure in the domain: `judgeRoute` (`src/domain/jev.ts`) applies the floor and cap from `catalog/jev.json`; `withinMaxEffort` (`src/domain/profile.ts`) and `withRoleBars` (`src/domain/catalog.ts`) shape what `candidates` (`src/domain/select.ts`) sees; `moveOffDown` and `nextStandIn` (`src/domain/failover.ts`) are built on the existing `rankStandIns`. A new `src/services/limits.ts` keeps the machine's down quotas in `<data>/limits.json`. `routing-service.ts` moves a pick off a down quota; `dispatch-service.ts` `failover()` retries, walks the stand-in chain and records each decision; admission accepts the stand-in failover chose.

**Tech Stack:** Bun ≥ 1.4, TypeScript, zod 4. No new dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-12-catherd-1.6-design.md`, "Plan 31: routing" (binding), with "Owner rulings needed" and "Not in 1.6". t3herd sources (read-only, `/Users/vigen/agora/lab/t3herd` at 71cbb6a): `src/jev.ts:84-92` `judgeAll`, `catalog/jev-route.json`, `src/core.ts:20-157`, `src/profile.ts:20-36, 130-152`, `src/models.ts:75-82`, `src/step.ts:485-497`, commits 22b8dbc, 9a0a384, 1f82801.

## Review Focus

1. **A provider is limited and no rung of the role's ladder stands in.** Expected: `route` keeps its pick and its `why` says the quota is limited with no stand-in on the ladder; the dispatch hits the limit and failover goes on to the profile's rungs and the catalog. Pinned in Task 4 ("keeps the pick and says so when no rung of the role stands in") and Task 5 ("goes on to the profile's rungs when the ladder has no stand-in").
2. **The profile's stand-in sits on a quota another run already found limited.** Expected: it is skipped for the next stand-in, not started into a second limit. Pinned in Task 5 ("skips the profile's stand-in when its quota was down before this limit").
3. **A provider error repeats.** Expected: one retry on the same rung, then failover; never a second retry, never a loop. Pinned in Task 5 ("retries a provider error once on the same rung, then fails over").
4. **The quota comes back.** Expected: an `ok` record on it clears the mark at once, and a mark older than 30 minutes no longer moves a route. Pinned in Task 4 ("reads a mark only while the hold lasts") and Task 5 ("clears a quota's mark when a role finishes ok on it").
5. **`limits.json` is unreadable** (a crash mid-write on an old catherd, a hand edit). Expected: routing and failover go on as if nothing were limited, and the next mark rewrites it. Pinned in Task 4 ("reads an unreadable file as no limit, and the next mark rewrites it").
6. **A `maxEffort` ceiling below a rung the profile names as a stand-in.** Expected: that stand-in is never started; the next one is. Pinned in Task 5 ("never starts a stand-in above its model's maxEffort").
